Where your data lives Nowhere In this browser In this browser On this device

All versions show the same views (day, 3/5/6 days, week, month), grades, average, absences, filters, .ics export and week/day images to share.

Your data stays with you

No server, no account, nothing stored on GitHub.

No server

The extension reads Odin in your browser and keeps everything in your browser. This site only hosts the code.

Encrypted links

A share link carries your data encrypted (AES-256). Without your share code it is unreadable. GitHub never receives it: the part after "#" never leaves the browser.

Chosen lifetime

You choose how long a link stays valid (1 hour to 7 days). After that, the app refuses it.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers. The full help (in French) has more than fifty, with search. Release notes are in French too.

Is it official?

No. It is a student project, not affiliated with Université Clermont Auvergne or the IUT. It simply reads the Odin pages you can already view yourself.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. There is no server and no account: the extension keeps everything in your browser, the app on your device. A share link carries your encrypted data and GitHub never receives it (the part after "#" stays in the browser).

Do I have to give my password?

No. You sign in on the university's real login page (CAS), with your two-factor authentication; the extension never sees your password. It then re-reads the Odin page that is displayed, like a copy-paste.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, with the app (Android, iPhone, offline). Data comes from the extension on your computer through an encrypted link, QR code or file. Direct sync on a phone will come with Firefox for Android.

What if Odin changes or goes down?

The extension notices: it shows a clear message and keeps your previous timetable instead of erasing it. A "site status" widget in Settings shows live what is answering.

Is it free?

Yes, with no ads and no account. The code is on GitHub.
